# Taller de Mateo: fondo y animación

Recursos nuevos: `assets/gba/ui/mateo_workshop_backdrop_v2.png` y `assets/gba/ui/mateo_pinion_v2.png`, generados con la herramienta integrada `imagegen`. Se conservan los recursos originales y la transparencia del piñón.

El fondo permanece fijo. `scripts/ui/forge_workbench_display.gd` dibuja por separado las regiones de la fragua y el fuelle existentes, el fuego animado, la rueda y sus refuerzos. El piñón de seis dientes gira en sentido contrario con una relación visual de 2:1. Los apoyos, las guías, el aire y el brillo de los remaches se dibujan en código. La rueda de madera mantiene su color; cambia el brillo del hierro.

Referencias existentes: `mateo_forge_bellows_sheet_v1.png`, `mill_gear_piece_v1.png` y `assets/gba/environment/forge_fire_sheet.png`.

## Prompt final del piñón

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: isolated 2D game sprite on TRUE alpha transparency. Image 1 is a STYLE reference only: a detailed historical wooden wheel with iron band. Create ONE smaller cast/forged iron pinion gear, FRONT VIEW, perfectly circular, centered in a 1024x1024 square canvas, exactly SIX evenly spaced chunky rectangular gear teeth around its circumference, round recessed metal hub and small square axle hole at center. Historical hand-forged dark charcoal-grey iron with warm muted highlights, convincing pitted metallic texture, beveled edge, subtle hammer marks and a few small rivets on the hub. Match the richly detailed crisp PIXEL ART of the provided wheel, matching dark outlines, lighting from upper left, resolution and palette, suitable to mesh with a wooden wheel on a blacksmith test stand. ONLY the isolated single iron pinion, entire object visible with narrow transparent margins; no wood, no stand, no supports, no floor, no ground shadow, no flames, no glow, no text, no lettering, no border. True transparent alpha, not checkerboard or solid background. Accurate SIX gear teeth, front-facing with no perspective distortion.

## Prompt final del fondo

Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: wide pixel-art background plate for an interactive blacksmith workshop in a historical educational game. Image 1 is an art style reference ONLY: the existing forge and leather bellows sprite sheet. Make a detailed WIDE 1536x640 landscape empty blacksmith workshop interior, near frontal view with slight top-down view of the floor, richly textured crisp pixel art, warm amber light and soot-dark stone walls, massive dark timber roof beams, small barred window in upper center, hanging iron tongs and horseshoe tools on the back wall, charcoal sacks and wood pile at far left edge, wooden workbench with scattered small hand tools along the FAR RIGHT wall, worn cobblestone floor across foreground. Layout must leave the CENTER and LEFT-CENTER foreground completely EMPTY for game sprites: the game will add a freestanding stone furnace with bellows on the left and a large wooden gear on a stand on the right. DO NOT draw a forge, fire, bellows, anvil, gear, wheel, large equipment, tub, barrel, people, characters, signs, UI or text anywhere. Background only. Warm dark brown, charcoal grey, gold palette matching the reference. Ground starts about 55 percent down the image; leave generous clear floor. No borders, no lettering, no overlays, no interfaces, no big objects in center. Atmosphere of a working traditional wheelwright and blacksmith shop.
