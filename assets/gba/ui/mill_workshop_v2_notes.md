## Prompts finales

### Taller

Wide detailed pixel-art background for a polished 19th century rural watermill adventure game, about 2.4:1 aspect ratio. Empty interior mill workshop, warm golden oak floor and beams, old stone walls, two small sunny windows, mill tools on rear wall, wooden sacks and grain storage only along far edges. Left wall has a water channel entrance, empty clear foreground. Main central floor and lower midground EMPTY so an animated watermill machine can be placed on top. No machinery, no waterwheel, no gears, no people, no text, no UI. Historical English village craft style, warm realistic pixel shading, crisp textures, amber light. Professional game backdrop, composition wide and legible.

### Estructura fija

Single isolated watermill stationary mechanism sprite, transparent background, detailed polished pixel-art historical RPG style, warm oak beams, iron brackets, limestone. Strict SIDE VIEW facing viewer, wide rectangular shape. Two vertical wooden supports and a horizontal oak beam form a sturdy frame. LEFT HALF mostly OPEN EMPTY SPACE, with a wooden water flume pouring trough at top-left and an empty stone water collecting basin below, to add an independently rotating large round wheel later. RIGHT HALF a cylindrical flat limestone millstone on a short timber platform at lower center-right, a vertical oak spindle above it, a wooden square EMPTY grain hopper at upper-right, a small wooden angled feed chute down toward stone center, and a short flour output chute to right. Millstone about x65% y70%, hopper x85% y30%. NO wheel attached, NO gears anywhere, NO water anywhere, NO flowing flour or grain, NO grain in hopper, NO sacks. No background, no floor outside frame, no labels. Entire structure fits image with margin and transparent cutout, no cast shadow. Emphasize visually rich oak grain and stone texture. This is a stationary sprite to be combined with independent wheel, gear and particle layers in game.

### Rueda hidráulica

One separate round WATERWHEEL sprite, isolated fully transparent background, perfectly FRONT-ON flat circular view so it can be rotated in game. Nineteenth century village oak watermill wheel, 12 chunky wooden paddle blocks evenly around outer perimeter, 8 radial spokes, dark iron axle hub and iron reinforcing rim. Warm golden oak, detailed hand-painted pixel-art historical RPG, crisp rich wood grain and iron bolts. Perfect circle, rotational symmetry, no isometric perspective, no ellipse. Fits 85 percent of square image centered. No water, no ground, no machinery, no shaft protruding outward, no background, no cast shadow, no text. Same polished style as classic historical adventure game craft workshops. Actual transparent alpha between spokes and around wheel.
